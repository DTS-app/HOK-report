⚠️ Issue 1 — Title Tag (CRITICAL)

The title tag is Google's most important on-page ranking signal for determining what the page is about.
🔴 Critical

Title Tag is empty of any keyword

Current Title
"All Bags"
✅ Recommended Title (58 characters)
Buy Bags Online in India | Vegan Leather Bags for Women – House of Kiyaara
✅ Alternative Title (55 characters)
Women's Bags Online India | Shop Vegan Leather Bags – House of Kiyaara
Why this matters: Every competitor includes "Buy", "Online", or "India" in their title. "All Bags" gives Google no signal about geography, purchase intent, or audience. Google simply cannot match your page to a "buy bags in India" search when the title says "All Bags".

🟠 Action Required

Schema 4: Product Schema on Individual Product Pages

Each individual product page (e.g. /products/vegan-leather-black-renata-tote-bag) should have a Product schema with:

Required fields: name, image, description, brand, offers (price, priceCurrency, availability, url), sku
Optional but powerful: aggregateRating (even 0 reviews can be shown as "No reviews yet"), material ("Vegan Leather"), color

Shopify with Rank Math or a JSON-LD app (like "JSON-LD for SEO" by Ilana Davis, free) can auto-generate this for all products. This is how products appear in Google Shopping results and product rich snippets — critical for a small brand to compete.
