01 · Executive Summary

The Big Picture

Root Cause Diagnosis

CTR Collapse, Not a Ranking Loss

Your impressions stayed strong (and even grew in March), but your click-through rate dropped sharply. This means Google is still showing your pages — but users are not choosing to click. The primary causes are: (1) your blog posts rank for high-volume "stole vs scarf" type queries but your titles/descriptions don't match what searchers want, (2) Google AI Overviews are answering those informational queries without users needing to click, and (3) one high-traffic blog has a 0.17% CTR on 8,734 impressions — an extraordinary waste.

03 · Root Cause Analysis

Why Did Users Stop Clicking?

After analyzing all 6 data files, here are the confirmed causes ranked by impact:

1

🤖 Google AI Overviews

Your most-shown keywords are informational queries like "stole vs scarf," "difference between scarf and stole," etc. Google's AI Overviews now answer these directly on the search page — no click needed.

"stole vs scarf" → 2,134 impressions, 4 clicks, 0.19% CTR
"scarf vs stole" → 578 impressions, 2 clicks, 0.35% CTR
"difference between scarf and stole" → 439 impr, 2 clicks
These queries get massive impressions but nearly ZERO clicks.
2

📉 One Blog Bleeding Traffic

Your "Scarves vs Stoles" blog has 8,734 impressions but only 15 clicks. That's a 0.17% CTR — catastrophic. It's ranking for the wrong intent or its title/meta doesn't convert.

URL: /blogs/blog/scarves-vs-stoles-which-one-complements-your-style
Impressions: 8,734 · Clicks: 15 · CTR: 0.17%
Position: 8.59 (Page 1!) — showing but not clicked at all.
3

🎯 Mismatch: Info Content, Commercial Brand

Your blog content ranks for "what is a stole," "twilly scarf meaning," "scarf sizes" — but these users are researchers, not buyers. They find informational answers and leave without clicking.

"twilly scarf" → 261 impressions, 0 clicks
"what is a stole scarf" → 248 impressions, 0 clicks
"scarf or stole" → 194 impressions, 0 clicks
Dozens of similar zero-click high-impression queries.
4

📍 Brand Clicks ≠ Organic Growth

Over 350 clicks come from branded queries ("kiyaara", "house of kiara"). These aren't new visitors — they already know you. Actual discovery traffic is much smaller than the total suggests.

"kiyaara" → 110 clicks · "house of kiara" → 103 clicks
"house of kiyaara" → 98 clicks
Combined branded: ~350+ clicks out of ~854 total
Non-branded organic = only ~500 clicks over 5+ months.
5

🌍 US Impressions Going Nowhere

The US is generating 13,584 impressions — your highest by far — but only 70 clicks (0.52% CTR). This suggests your content appears for US queries but doesn't resonate or is outcompeted by local brands.

United States: 13,584 impressions · 70 clicks · 0.52% CTR
India: 7,705 impressions · 571 clicks · 7.41% CTR
UAE: 368 impressions · 39 clicks · 10.6% CTR
US potential is massively untapped.
6

📱 Mobile Dominates, Desktop Underperforms

Mobile has 573 clicks at avg position 7.58. Desktop has 273 clicks at position 15.32. Desktop rankings are much weaker — likely affecting higher-intent shoppers who browse on desktop.

Mobile CTR: 2.75% (position 7.58)
Desktop CTR: 1.57% (position 15.32)
Tablet CTR: 0.95% (position 8.6)
Desktop ranking improvement = higher value shoppers.

08 · Action Plan

What To Do — Priority Order

Every action below is tied directly to a specific data finding. Do these in order for maximum impact:

  • P1
    Rewrite the Scarves vs Stoles blog title + meta description immediately This single page has 8,734 impressions and 0.17% CTR. Even improving to 2% CTR = 175 more clicks/month from one change. Make the title more emotional and specific: e.g., "Scarves vs Stoles: Which One Actually Works for Your Outfit?" Add a meta description that promises a specific answer + mentions your brand. → /blogs/blog/scarves-vs-stoles-which-one-complements-your-style
  • P1
    Add a "Featured Snippet Block" to your stole vs scarf blogs Google AI Overviews pull from pages that have clean Q&A structured content. Add a 40-60 word direct answer box at the very top of the blog: "A scarf is [X]. A stole is [Y]. The key difference is [Z]." This makes Google use YOUR content in the overview, which still increases brand exposure even without a click. → All "scarf vs stole" blogs
  • P1
    Fix the "Tuyoor Taoos" blog — rename or reposition it 683 impressions, 0 clicks, position 3.36. The word "Tuyoor Taoos" means nothing to someone searching Google. Change the title to something like: "The Art Behind Kiyaara's Tuyoor Taoos: Why This Collection Sells Out." This converts brand searchers AND educates new visitors. → /blogs/blog/why-tuyoor-taoos-is-the-perfect-blend-of-fashion-and-art
  • P2
    Create a dedicated "Eco-Friendly Handbags India" landing page "Environmentally friendly handbags" gets 293 impressions at position 38. You need a page built specifically for this commercial keyword. Create /collections/eco-friendly-vegan-handbags — optimized with this keyword in title, H1, and product descriptions. This is a buying-intent keyword with real revenue potential. → Create new: /collections/eco-friendly-vegan-handbags
  • P2
    Optimize all collection pages for Desktop ranking Desktop positions average 15.32 — page 2. Desktop users are more likely to complete a purchase. Improve page speed, internal linking between collections, and add unique keyword-rich descriptions to each collection page (/collections/bags, /collections/tote-bags, etc.). → /collections/bags · /collections/tote-bags · /collections/crossbody-bags
  • P2
    Build 3–5 backlinks to the Vegan Leather Handbags blog (position 18) "/how-innovative-vegan-leather-handbags..." is stuck at position 18. It's almost on page 2. A few quality backlinks (fashion directories, ethical fashion blogs, guest posts) would push it to page 1, where CTR jumps from ~1% to 5–15%. → /blogs/blog/how-innovative-vegan-leather-handbags-are-changing-sustainable-fashion
  • P3
    Target UAE and GCC with dedicated content UAE has 10.6% CTR — your best non-branded country. Create content and product collections specifically for the GCC market: "Best Vegan Leather Bags in Dubai," "Hand-Painted Luxury Bags UAE." These searchers convert. Promote the content on UAE-based Instagram accounts. → New blog + collection targeting GCC keywords
  • P3
    Set up monthly GSC monitoring — track CTR by page Going forward, create a monthly Google Sheets tracker. For each key URL, log: impressions, clicks, CTR, and position. Any page with 500+ impressions and under 1% CTR goes on your fix list. This prevents a repeat of March — where a problem built quietly for weeks before being noticed. → GSC → Search Results → Pages → Export monthly
